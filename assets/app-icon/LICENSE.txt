LayerShop app icon (photocraft.svg, photocraft-small.svg and every file derived from them in
this directory: photocraft-1024.png, photocraft.icns, photocraft.ico, hicolor/).

Original artwork made for LayerShop by Slavomir Durej: three stacked sheets on a dark tile,
drawn by a script (tools/make-app-icon.py in the LayerShop website project). No third-party
material. It replaces the app icon of upstream PhotoCraft in this fork. Licensed like the rest of
